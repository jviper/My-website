Structural Integrity

Is Your Deck Structurally Sound, or Just Standing?

Most contractors focus strictly on the surface aesthetics. However, water ingress, ledger board decay, and failing concrete footings are silent threats. A wobbly railing or a soft joist is not a cosmetic issue—it is a structural liability. We do not apply band-aid fixes. We strip the assembly down to the framing and engineer the solution before laying the finish.

Technical Competence

Engineered Repair Scopes

Structural Framing & Ledger Repair

Diagnosis and replacement of rotted joists, decayed rim boards, and compromised ledger attachments to ensure maximum load-bearing capacity.

Surface Replacement & Upgrades

Complete removal of failing organic materials. Transitioning your structural footprint to high-yield composite decking with hidden fastener architecture.

Footing & Foundation Stabilization

Correcting settling issues, reinforcing substructures, and pouring high-strength concrete footings to stop structural migration.

Custom Finish Carpentry

Integration of rigid stair stringers, modernized railing parameters, and low-voltage electrical lighting systems for immediate aesthetic authority.
