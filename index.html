What We Do

Comprehensive Deck Repair & Restoration

From minor fixes to complete overhauls, we deliver precision workmanship that extends the life of your outdoor space.

🔧

Structural Repair

Rotted joists, loose railings, wobbly stairs — we reinforce and replace compromised structural components to make your deck safe and solid again.

🪥

Power Washing & Cleaning

Deep cleaning that strips away years of grime, mold, and mildew, prepping the surface perfectly for the next step.

🖌️

Staining & Sealing

Premium stains and sealers that protect against UV rays, moisture, and wear — bringing out the natural beauty of the wood.

🪵

Board Replacement

Damaged, cracked, or warped boards carefully removed and replaced with matching lumber for a seamless look.

✨

Sanding & Refinishing

We sand away splinters and rough patches, restoring a smooth, like-new surface ready for family gatherings.

🏗️

Railing & Stair Upgrades

Modernize with new railing systems, balusters, and stair treads that elevate both safety and curb appeal.
